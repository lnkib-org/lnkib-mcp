# Example prompts

Use a key with the scopes noted in brackets.

**Create a bio draft** `[bio:write]`
> Create a Link-in-Bio draft for my bakery "Mina's Baking Studio" with these links: class booking https://example.com/class, recipes https://example.com/recipe. Add my Instagram @mina.bakes and TikTok @mina. Keep it private and show me the result.

**Review before publishing** `[bio:read]`
> List my bio pages and tell me which one is a draft and what is missing before I publish it.

**Publish after you checked it** `[bio:publish]`
> Publish the bio page "mina-bakes". I reviewed it.

**Read bookings** `[bookings:read]`
> Show my pending bookings and tell me which days are busiest. Do not repeat customer contact details.

**Add a manual booking** `[bookings:write]`
> Add a manual booking: Jia Kim, 2026-11-20 at 14:00 for 30 minutes, note "beginner class for 2".
